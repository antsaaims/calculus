$$
f(x) = F'(x)
$$

I'm the **rate of change** — the [[Derivative]] of a real-world
[[Quantity Function F(x)|quantity function]] $F$. I don't tell you *how much* there is;
I tell you *how fast it's changing*, at every input.

**Math example:** if $F(x) = x^2$ (miles at hour $x$), then $f(x) = F'(x) = 2x$, so
$f(3) = 6$ miles per hour.

**Real-world example:** if $F(t)$ is the gallons of water in a tank, $f(t)$ is the
gallons per minute flowing in (positive) or draining out (negative) right now.
