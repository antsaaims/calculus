I'm the **tangent line** — I touch a curve at exactly one point and lean in the exact
same direction the curve is heading right there.

My slope *is* the [[Derivative]] of the function at that point. Zoom in close enough on
any smooth curve, and it starts looking just like me.

**Math example:** the tangent to $y = x^2$ at $x = 1$ has slope $2$, giving the line
$y = 2x - 1$.

**Real-world example:** steering a car around a curve, the direction you're pointed at
any instant matches the tangent to the road's curve at that exact point.
