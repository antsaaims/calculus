My graph is a picture of every [[Input and Output]] pair I produce — plot the input on
the $x$-axis, the output on the $y$-axis, and connect the dots.

Read my graph left to right and you can see everything: where I'm positive or negative
([[Sign of a Function]]), where I'm going up or down, and where I bend.

**Math example:** the graph of $f(x) = x^2$ is a parabola through $(0,0)$, $(-2,4)$,
and $(2,4)$ — plot those pairs and connect the dots.

**Real-world example:** a graph of outdoor temperature over 24 hours lets you spot the
hottest moment of the day just by finding the highest point on the curve.
