You don't need to compute anything to read a function's [[Sign of a Function|sign]] off
its [[Graph]]:

- **Above the $x$-axis** $\Rightarrow$ positive ($> 0$)
- **Below the $x$-axis** $\Rightarrow$ negative ($< 0$)
- **On the $x$-axis** $\Rightarrow$ zero

Just look at where the curve sits.

**Math example:** the graph of $f(x) = x^2 - 4$ dips below the $x$-axis between
$x = -2$ and $x = 2$, so $f$ is negative on that stretch.

**Real-world example:** a graph of a bank balance dipping below the $x$-axis means the
account is overdrawn during that stretch of time.
