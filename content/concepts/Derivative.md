---
title: Derivative
description: The derivative of a function is its instantaneous rate of change — the slope of its tangent line at a point.
tags:
  - functions
---

The **derivative** of a function tells you how fast that function is changing at an
exact instant. It's the slope of the [[Tangent]] line at a point on the [[Graph]].

$$
f(x) = F'(x)
$$

A [[Rate of change|rate of change]] function is just a derivative applied to a real-world
quantity — same math, real-world meaning.
