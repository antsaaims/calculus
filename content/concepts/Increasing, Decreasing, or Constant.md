---
title: Increasing, Decreasing, or Constant
description: A function is increasing where it rises left to right, decreasing where it falls, constant where it holds steady.
tags:
  - functions
---

Read a graph left to right:

- **Increasing** — the graph rises.
- **Decreasing** — the graph falls.
- **Constant** — the graph holds steady, flat.

See [[Recognizing a Max or Min]] for what happens right where a function switches
between these.
