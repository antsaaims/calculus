---
title: Sign of a Function
description: A function's value can be positive, negative, or zero at any given input.
tags:
  - functions
---

At any input, a function's output is either **positive** ($> 0$), **negative**
($< 0$), or exactly **zero**. That's its sign at that point.

See [[Reading Sign from a Graph]] for how to spot the sign just by looking at where the
graph sits relative to the $x$-axis.
