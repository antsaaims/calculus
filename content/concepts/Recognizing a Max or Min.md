---
title: Recognizing a Max or Min
description: Increase then decrease means a max; decrease then increase means a min.
tags:
  - functions
---

You can spot a [[Min and Max|relative max or min]] just from the pattern around it:

- **Increase, then decrease** $\Rightarrow$ that turning point is a relative **max**.
- **Decrease, then increase** $\Rightarrow$ that turning point is a relative **min**.

No formula needed — just watch which way the graph is heading right before and right
after the point.
