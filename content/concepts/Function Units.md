---
title: Function Units
description: The units of a quantity function and its rate of change are linked — rate units are quantity units per x-unit.
tags:
  - functions
---

Every real-world [[Quantity Function F(x)|quantity function]] carries units — dollars,
miles, people. Its [[Derivative|rate of change]] carries related units: the same
quantity units, but **per** unit of input.

**Example:** if $F$ is measured in miles, and $x$ in hours, then $f = F'$ is measured in
miles *per hour*.
