---
title: Reading Sign from a Graph
description: Above the x-axis means positive, below means negative — no computation needed.
tags:
  - functions
  - graph-reading
---

You don't need to compute anything to read a function's [[Sign of a Function|sign]] off
its [[Graph]]:

- **Above the $x$-axis** $\Rightarrow$ positive ($> 0$)
- **Below the $x$-axis** $\Rightarrow$ negative ($< 0$)
- **On the $x$-axis** $\Rightarrow$ zero

Just look at where the curve sits.
