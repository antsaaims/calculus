---
title: Extrema of the Rate of Change
description: Where the rate-of-change graph has its own max or min, the quantity function F has an inflection point (its fastest change).
tags:
  - rate-of-change
  - graph-reading
---

This one trips people up, so slow down: we're looking at a max or min **on the RoC graph
itself** — not on $F$.

Where $f$ reaches its own highest or lowest point, that's the moment $F$ is changing
**fastest** — an **inflection point** of $F$, where concavity switches.

- $f$ has a max $\Rightarrow$ $F$ is increasing as fast as it ever will, right there.
- $f$ has a min $\Rightarrow$ $F$ is decreasing as fast as it ever will, right there.

Compare this to [[x-Intercepts of RoC]], which is about a completely different feature
of the same graph.
